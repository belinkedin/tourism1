Kodaikanal — Tamil for “Gift of the Forest” — sits at 2,133 metres in the Palani Hills of Tamil Nadu’s Western Ghats. Established in 1845, this misty hill station enchants visitors with its star-shaped lake, towering Pillar Rocks, fragrant pine forests, Silver Cascade waterfall, and the mystical Guna Caves. Whether you seek the romance of dawn mist or the thrill of valley viewpoints — Kodai delivers it all.
